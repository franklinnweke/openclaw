# Franklin Portfolio Proof

Use this skill when Franklin asks for portfolio updates, weekly proof, case
study drafts, public-safe client progress, job-search proof points, or evidence
of agentic workflow competence.

## Positioning rules

Franklin's public positioning:

- Web Systems and AI Automation Engineer.
- Product-minded systems builder.
- Practical business systems with credible AI capability underneath.
- No fake terminal theatrics.
- No inflated autonomy claims.
- No private client data.

## Public-safe client framing

Allowed:

- "commerce/operations platform for a fashion/tailoring client"
- "luxury rental/concierge booking and client-ops product"
- "workflow automation, approval gates, QA, integrations, and operational
  tooling"

Avoid:

- private revenue, customer, booking, payment, or contact details,
- internal credentials,
- screenshots with personal/client data,
- claims that an agent made final business decisions.

## Required proof packet

Produce:

1. `headline`: one sentence.
2. `what_changed`: bullets tied to commits, screenshots, docs, or shipped
   features.
3. `why_it_matters`: business or operator value.
4. `evidence`: links, file paths, screenshots, test output, or run history.
5. `public_safe_case_study`: concise narrative.
6. `risk_notes`: what is intentionally not disclosed or automated.
7. `next_actions`: small concrete follow-ups.

## Evidence standards

- Prefer commit links, local file paths, screenshots, QA output, and issue/PR
  references.
- Label inferred impact as inference.
- Do not turn private implementation details into public claims.
- If evidence is missing, say what must be captured next.

## Tone

Write like a pragmatic engineer explaining real operational value. Avoid hype,
agent buzzwords without evidence, and broad claims like "fully autonomous."
